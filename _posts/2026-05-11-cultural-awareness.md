<h2>What is cultural awareness</h2>

Cultural awareness is when you're aware that people from different cultures have differences that are respectable and accepting differences without being a jerk. Building cultural awareness can help you have meaningful interactions with people around you in a multicultural, cosmopolitan environment like Australia where there can be unfamiliar experiences.
This can help if:
- you want to learn how to understand other cultures
- you want to learn how to talk to people with different backgrounds
- you want to be more open-minded when doing both

<h2>How to build cultural awareness</h2>

<h3>Talk to someone</h3>

Probably communicating with people from different backgrounds is the second most fun way to get to know different cultures, talking to people will surround you with friends that you can rely on. If you're more familiar with meeting people online, you can try to chat up a person who want to get to know better in the community Discord server who has the same hobbies as you or look for international penpals to look forward to writing to. This can also help you practise your conversational or writing language skills when learning a new language. Remember to treat them as a friend rather than a way to build your cultural awareness, the goal is to not treat people differently but to make them feel welcome about talking to someone different.

<h3>Travel</h3>

Travelling is the first most fun way to get to know different cultures. You're surrounding yourself in an unfamiliar environment with an unfamiliar community. Getting to travel gives you an entirely new experience you might enjoy while learning different cultures. If you can't save up to travel anywhere, look for a virtual or documented travel experience from someone else on social media going to your chosen country.

<h3>Do research</h3>

One of the worst things you can do while trying to understand people is getting it from an unreliable secondary source, presumably from spoken rumors aroused from certain events or stereotypes which refuse to let go. Stereotypes can affect different people heavily as it will limit their opportunities and quality of life internationally. Instead, make an effort and do research to get the learn people and share what you found out to social media to hopefully get more people to understand a specific culture in a truthier way.
